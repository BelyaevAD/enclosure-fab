Template `strip_clip` is implemented in [`fabkit/strip_clip.py`](../../fabkit/strip_clip.py).

One channel for a body that slides in from the front. Pocket, band, wall, and teeth come from the spec. Two teeth, one hole in the top bar, part lying on its side. The hole is a teardrop so it prints without support.

Ready recipe: [`docs/patterns.md`](../../docs/patterns.md). Do not punch an enclosure. Do not copy the ring template. Do not go back to a single tooth.
